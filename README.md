# 🚀 NEXCT Terminal

**NEXCT Terminal** is a modern cryptocurrency trading terminal designed to bring market insights, trading signals, and crypto research into one interactive dashboard.

### ✨ Features

- 📊 **Market Analytics** — Explore cryptocurrency market movements and price trends.
- 📈 **Top Gainers & Losers** — Identify cryptocurrencies with notable 24-hour price changes.
- 🎯 **Trading Signals** — View LONG/SHORT setups, entry zones, stop-loss levels, take-profit targets, and conviction scores.
- 📰 **Market Updates** — Stay informed with curated cryptocurrency and macroeconomic news.
- 🔬 **Crypto Research** — Discover research papers and insights related to blockchain, cryptocurrency, and market behavior.
- 🌊 **Season Index** — Access additional market sentiment and ecosystem information.
- ⚡ **Interactive Dashboard** — Explore multiple categories of crypto market information through a unified interface.
